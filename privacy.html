Privacy & cookie policy

Effective September 19, 2026. This policy describes FeedPetsWell by Calm and Simple at feedpetswell.com.

Your calculator entries

Calculations run in your browser. FeedPetsWell does not submit your pet’s weight, feeding choices or calculation results to a server or to Google Analytics. Entries are not saved when you leave the page. If you copy or share a result, you choose where to send it.

Hosting and technical information

GitHub Pages hosts this website. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times and browser information for delivery and security under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Optional analytics and your choices

Google Analytics loads only after you select “Allow analytics.” It helps us understand page usage and may use cookies and technical identifiers. Rejecting analytics leaves the calculator fully usable. Your choice is stored locally in your browser under “feedpetswell-analytics” until you change it or clear browser data. Use Cookie preferences in the footer to change your choice. Revoking consent stops future Analytics loading and removes accessible Analytics cookies set for this site; it does not erase data already processed by Google. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s Analytics opt-out tool.

Advertising

Google AdSense ads are not active on this version of FeedPetsWell. If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. Google advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalise ads using browsing activity across sites.

You can manage personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center and participating third-party choices at AboutAds. Learn how Google uses information from partner sites. The policy and applicable consent controls will be updated before ads are activated.

Purchases, downloads and external sites

Gumroad processes guide orders, payment details and delivery information under its privacy policy. FeedPetsWell does not receive card details through this website. Sellers may receive order and customer details through Gumroad for fulfilment and support. Amazon affiliate links may let Amazon attribute a purchase to us under Amazon’s privacy notice.

The optional free-resource collection opens Google Forms. Information you choose to submit is processed through Google and the form owner for the purpose described on that form. The calculator requires no email address. External providers may process data outside your country and apply their own retention rules.

Requests and updates

For questions or a request relating to information you submitted, use the contact page. Do not post private data in public GitHub issues; purchase questions should use Gumroad’s private receipt support channel. This service is intended for adult pet caregivers. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.
