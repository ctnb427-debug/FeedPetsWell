FEEDPETSWELL / THE DETAILS

A little clarity for pet parents.

FeedPetsWell is a free cat and dog feeding calculator from Calm and Simple, created by Collette. It brings feeding estimates and printable pet-care resources together in one easy-to-use place.

What you can expect

Free access to the calculator, no account required, and an explanation of the formula behind the numbers. Optional digital guides and affiliate links help support this project.

Our limits

FeedPetsWell is an independent educational project, not a veterinary service. We do not claim veterinary review or endorsement. Calculated portions are starting estimates, not diagnoses or treatment plans.

We use published veterinary references for the calculation and distinguish educational content from products for sale. See our methodology and disclosures.
