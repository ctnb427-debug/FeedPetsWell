10 useful things for happier, healthier pets

Not a list of every gadget on the internet. These are the everyday items that genuinely help with feeding, water, calm and a cleaner home, and they make lovely Christmas gifts for the pet lovers in your life.

As an Amazon Associate, FeedPetsWell earns from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. It helps keep the calculator free.

Hydration

Cat water fountain

Many cats drink more from moving water than from a still bowl. A quiet fountain with a replaceable filter keeps water fresh all day, which is especially helpful for cats on dry food.

Look for: a quiet pump, easy-to-clean parts, and stainless steel or ceramic if your cat gets chin acne.

Feeding

Slow feeder bowl

For dogs (and some cats) who inhale their dinner. Ridges and mazes slow eating down, so meals last longer and fewer come back up.

Look for: dishwasher-safe, non-slip base, and a pattern sized for your pet's snout.

See options on Amazon.ca ↗

Portions

Digital food scale

Cups vary a lot. Weighing food in grams is the most accurate way to serve the amount the FeedPetsWell calculator gives you, and it's the best tool for gentle weight loss.

Look for: grams display, a tare button, and at least 1 g accuracy.

See options on Amazon.ca ↗

Feeding

Automatic pet feeder

Serves measured meals on a schedule, so breakfast arrives on time even when you sleep in, and portions stay consistent when someone else is pet-sitting.

Look for: portion control in small amounts, a battery backup and a lid that locks.

See options on Amazon.ca ↗

Calm & enrichment

Lick mat

Spread on a little wet food, yogurt or pumpkin and freeze. Licking is soothing for anxious pets, and it makes a small amount of food last.

Look for: food-grade silicone, a suction base and dishwasher-safe.

See options on Amazon.ca ↗

Calm & enrichment

Snuffle mat

Hide kibble in the fabric folds and let your pet sniff it out. It turns part of a meal into a brain game, which is perfect for rainy or snowy days indoors.

Look for: machine-washable and a non-slip bottom.

See options on Amazon.ca ↗

Treats

Churu lickable cat treats

One of Amazon's best-selling pet items for a reason: most cats go wild for them. They're handy for hiding pills too. Count them toward the daily calorie total.

Tip: one tube is about 6 kcal, so they fit into most daily budgets.

See options on Amazon.ca ↗

Comfort

Orthopedic pet bed

Memory-foam beds support older joints and big dogs that sprawl. A favourite Christmas gift for a senior pet.

Look for: a removable washable cover, a waterproof liner and the right size (measure your pet stretched out).

See options on Amazon.ca ↗

Home

Muddy paws door mat

Super-absorbent microfibre that traps wet paws at the door. Essential for Canadian winters, slush and spring mud.

Look for: machine-washable, a rubber backing and a size that fits your entryway.

See options on Amazon.ca ↗

Home

Reusable pet hair remover

A roller that lifts pet hair off sofas, blankets and car seats with no sticky sheets to buy. Simply open it and empty the hair.

Look for: a self-cleaning roller design and a sturdy handle.

Before you buy

Products can't replace veterinary care. If your pet has a health condition, ask your vet which bowls, feeders, treats or beds suit them best. And if you're unsure how much to put in that new bowl, try the free FeedPetsWell calculator.
